What to confirm for your purchase

  • Compare fixed-mechanism and SL-track configurations rather than assuming that every chair follows the same back-massage path.
  • Check upright and reclined dimensions, doorway access and operating clearance for the intended home setting.
  • Confirm controller language, heating zones, foot functions, packaging and after-sales scope for the selected chair.
Are all home massage chairs in this collection the same specification?

No. The catalogue groups 10 models for home-range sourcing. For example, White Home Comfort lists a fixed massage mechanism and 5 automatic programmes; ZZ316 lists an SL track and 12 automatic programmes. Compare their individual pages and approve the selected configuration.

What should I send for a wholesale quote?

Send the model, quantity, destination market and port, branding requirements and whether you need a sample. MOQ, sample charges, price, production timing and warranty scope are confirmed for the agreed project.
