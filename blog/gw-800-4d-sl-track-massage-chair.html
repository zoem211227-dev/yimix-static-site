GW-800 4D SL Track Massage Chair

These showroom photos show the chair upright and reclined, with close-ups of the controller, armrest buttons and separate leg sections. For distributors and showroom buyers, the photos give you a reference for the demo. Use the configuration details below to decide what to verify on a sample.

GW-800 configuration at a glance

Massage mechanism and track
4D mechanism with a 127 cm SL track.
Controls and programs
7-inch LCD controller, rotary control and 12 automatic programs. The sheet lists five mechanism speeds, five intensity levels and three air-pressure levels.
Recline and leg section
Two zero-gravity positions and 160 mm of powered foot extension.
Electrical configuration on the sheet
220 V AC, 50 Hz and 128 W rated power. Confirm voltage, frequency, plug and labeling for the destination market.
Dimensions and weight
Upright 157 × 79 × 108 cm. Reclined 165 × 79 × 106.5 cm. Listed net weight 106 kg.
Packing dimensions
Main carton 167.5 × 87 × 92.5 cm. Leg carton 64 × 56.5 × 62.5 cm. Confirm the final carton count and weights before arranging delivery.

Check the controller and shoulder scan on a sample

Start the chair from its mounted controller and follow the shoulder-height detection prompt. Ask the operator to show how to adjust the shoulder position after the scan. Then run an automatic program and try the armrest controls from the seat. The photographed screen shows Chinese. Request a demonstration in the language your customers need before approving the configuration.

Run a GW-800 showroom demo before training your sales team

Give a staff member who has never used the sample three tasks: start a program, find the stop control, and return the chair to its starting position. Have a second person observe from beside the chair and record the steps that needed help. Repeat with the controller language you plan to sell in once the factory has confirmed that configuration in writing.

Record what the next salesperson needs to know

Use one row per task: sample ID, display language, starting screen, expected result, actual result, and the reference photo or clip. For example, if a tester can find the shoulder adjustment only after a colleague points it out, capture the screen and add that step to the demo card. Repeat the task with a new tester before approving the card.

When you review the next unit, use the same sequence. If the screen labels or steps differ, record the unit identifier and confirm whether the control instructions need a revision before reusing the original card. For a broader review of menus and controls across models, see the controller usability guide.
