How to Compare Massage Chair Supplier Quotes on the Same Specification

Compare massage chair quotations by configuration, quantity, branding, packing, freight responsibilities, sample charges and after-sales scope.

YIMIX Buyer Resources · Published 16 October 2026 · Based on the linked model specifications and procurement review questions.

The short answer

Compare the same chair configuration, quantity and commercial scope before comparing unit prices. Record excluded or unconfirmed items explicitly, and keep first-order costs separate from repeat-order assumptions. A low unit price is not a complete purchasing comparison.

In this guide
  1. Issue one RFQ revision to each supplier
  2. Normalize the comparison fields
  3. Check the packing basis before estimating freight
  4. Show first-order and repeat-order assumptions separately
  5. Resolve differences before accepting the quotation

Issue one RFQ revision to each supplier

Begin with a named model or a clear required configuration. State the mechanism, track, programmes, controls, voltage, accessories, logo and packaging requirements. Include the quantity, destination and whether you are requesting a sample or a production quotation. Ask suppliers to identify alternatives rather than silently treating them as equivalent.

If the model is not yet chosen, create a shortlist first. The YIMIX model comparison helps separate actual configurations. For instance, the six-head and True 4D examples have different mechanisms, programme counts and packing arrangements; a price for one should not be presented as a quotation for the other.

Normalize the comparison fields

PackingCartons per chair, dimensions, gross weight and separate componentsEstablish a comparable logistics basis

After-salesWritten warranty scope, parts and support arrangementsAvoid treating vague support claims as identical

Use the same currency and comparison date. If a field is pending, mark it pending; a blank cell is not a zero cost or a promise that the item is included.

Check the packing basis before estimating freight

Product weight and package weight serve different purposes. Request the complete carton arrangement for the selected chair. The 6-Head SL Track model lists 75 / 92 kg net / gross weight and 129 × 79 × 108 cm package dimensions. The True 4D model lists a main package and a separate leg-unit package. Compare complete shipments, not just one carton from each quotation.

Use confirmed packing and route-specific information from the supplier and freight provider. Keep handling, delivery access and destination services visible in the comparison. The shipping checklist explains which packing details to request.

Show first-order and repeat-order assumptions separately

List the sample, branding/setup, product, inspection, packing and logistics items relevant to your project. Ask which charges recur, which are one-time and which depend on a future quantity or approval. Do not assume a sample is refundable, a setup is reusable or branding is free unless those conditions are written in the quotation.

This guide does not publish a universal YIMIX MOQ, price, payment schedule or production lead time. Those terms require confirmation for the selected model and scope. Retain the original quotation beside your comparison worksheet so buyer assumptions cannot be mistaken for supplier commitments.

Resolve differences before accepting the quotation

Send unresolved rows back to the supplier. Request a revised written quotation where specifications, exclusions or packing assumptions have changed. Record validity dates and the approval reference. The purchase order should point to the same configuration and commercial scope you compared.

Use the RFQ comparison CSV template to organize supplier-confirmed values, pending items and evidence. After selecting the configuration, share the model, quantity, destination and branding brief through the YIMIX inquiry form.

Buyer questions

Can I compare two suppliers by unit price alone?

Only after checking that the configuration, quantity, packing and included commercial scope match. Record differences and exclusions before deciding.

Are sample charges automatically credited against a bulk order?

Do not assume that. Confirm any credit amount, qualifying order and conditions in the written quotation.
