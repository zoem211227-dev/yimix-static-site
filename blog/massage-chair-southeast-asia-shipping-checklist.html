Importing Massage Chairs to Southeast Asia: A Buyer’s Pre-Shipment Checklist

YIMIX Buyer Resources · 16 November 2026

Choosing a massage chair supplier is only the first step. Before a shipment leaves the factory, buyers need to know which models are in each carton, how the electrical configuration matches the destination, and which records they will use when goods arrive. These details matter whether you are evaluating an initial order or replenishing an established retail range.

Start with a model and destination brief

Send the factory the model references, intended destination, proposed quantities and sales channel. Ask which specifications apply to the exact configuration, including controller language, plug, voltage and frequency. Requirements vary by destination and product; confirm applicable import and electrical requirements with your local qualified adviser before ordering.

If you intend to test the market with a small quantity, describe the proposed quantity and ask for a written answer on availability, customization and pricing. Do not assume that a sample or a particular order size is automatically available for every model.

Match each chair to its carton record

For each model and revision, request the carton count per chair, packed dimensions, gross weight, carton markings and included accessories. A leg unit or other component may be packed separately, so reconcile chairs and cartons before comparing shipping arrangements. Ask the supplier which packaging specification applies to your order rather than assuming that all models use identical cartons.

A useful buyer record has five fields: model and revision; number of complete chairs; number of cartons; packing reference; and open questions. Keep the record with the quotation and purchase order. If a change is approved after a sample, record exactly which configuration changed and which document supersedes the earlier version.

Review loading and receiving evidence

The supplied photographs show cartons in a container and chairs on a factory line. They can help buyers discuss packaging and production, but a photograph alone cannot establish the final destination, the number of chairs shipped or the result of an inspection. Request project-specific carton counts and shipping documents for your own order.

Before dispatch, agree who supplies the packing list and which photographs or inspection records will be available. At arrival, count cartons against the packing list, note visible damage before unpacking, and retain photographs and carton labels. Record any missing component against the chair model and carton number; discuss discrepancies with the supplier and carrier according to the agreed terms.

Compare the full purchasing scope

Ask for a written quotation that identifies the chair configuration, packaging basis, trade terms, freight responsibilities and destination charges. Clarify who arranges insurance and import clearance. The chair price alone does not describe the landed cost; local transport, taxes, storage and handling may also matter. Confirm the applicable trade terms and responsibilities for your transaction with your supplier and logistics provider.

For after-sales planning, ask which spare parts can be identified for the chosen model, what documents and troubleshooting information are available, and which warranty and service terms apply in writing. Do not assume a general product description is a warranty.

Prepare your inquiry

Share your destination country, shortlisted models, proposed quantity, preferred voltage and plug, and any branding or packaging needs. Ask YIMIX to confirm the model-specific configuration, sample options and commercial terms for your project.
