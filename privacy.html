Privacy & optional analytics

Updated: October 16, 2026

Website analytics

If you accept optional analytics, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand page visits and interactions such as WhatsApp link clicks, telephone and email link clicks, product-list downloads and starting an inquiry. Google may process device/browser information, approximate location and analytics identifiers. Analytics cookies are enabled only after you choose “Accept analytics”. You can reject optional analytics or change your choice using “Analytics preferences” at the bottom of any page.

Our custom events do not send your inquiry name, company, email, telephone, message or uploaded reference files to Google Analytics. We remove URL query strings and fragments from the page and referrer values used by this integration. Google Signals, advertising personalization and automatic enhanced measurement are disabled in this setup.

Learn how Google uses information from sites using its services or visit Google’s browser opt-out tool. No optional analytics script is loaded before your consent.

WhatsApp inquiries

The inquiry form prepares a WhatsApp message for you to review and send. Opening WhatsApp does not mean your inquiry has been sent or received. The website has no inquiry database, email delivery or CRM connection. Information you choose to send through WhatsApp is processed by WhatsApp under its own policies.
